# Organic Chemistry 1 – Final Exam Review

Study materials I put together while preparing for my Organic Chemistry 1 class, following *Organic Chemistry* by John McMurry (Chapters 1–13). They cover structure and bonding, acids and bases, alkanes and cycloalkanes, stereochemistry, alkenes, alkynes, organohalides, substitution and elimination reactions, mass spectrometry, IR, and NMR spectroscopy.

Each chapter has a study sheet, flashcards, and ACS-style practice questions with answers and explanations, plus a 50-question mock final drawn from the whole book.
